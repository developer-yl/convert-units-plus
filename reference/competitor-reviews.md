# Converter app review complaints: evidence and alternatives

Publisher-authored by Adysseus. Checked 2026-10-26.

Retrieved up to 50 most-recent written US App Store reviews per competitor from Apple’s public customer-review feed. Screened 1–3-star records for relevant comparison topics and inspected selected examples alongside current listings, release notes and visible developer replies. Every rating in the returned sample contributes to sampleSize; only selected critical examples are paraphrased below. These are qualitative examples, not a representative survey, a defect rate or a cross-app quality ranking. Dates displayed in the article use Asia/Singapore; raw Apple timestamps and versions are retained.

User-reported experiences; current reproduction and resolution are not verified unless explicitly stated. The RSS feed does not supply developer replies; contextual replies come from the linked public App Store page and may concern other, older reviews.

Conversion Calculator Plus documents one-minute active Currency+ checks. Four rivals explicitly state slower automatic schedules: Units Plus daily; Currency converter calculator! and GlobeConvert hourly; Converter+ every six hours or on request. The other seven reviewed descriptions do not specify a numeric automatic interval.

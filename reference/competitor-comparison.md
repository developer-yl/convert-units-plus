## Conversion Calculator Plus: product facts

Publisher: Adysseus. App ID: 1113920724. Checked: 2026-10-26.

- 72 conversion categories and 685 non-currency units; category counts describe product organization, not an independent quality score.
- Measurement conversion, currency/crypto/precious-metal comparison and an expression calculator for supported converters.
- Currency+ compares several currencies in one list and checks rates every minute while its rates page is visible, the app is active and internet is connected.
- A rate check does not guarantee a new market observation. The one-minute policy does not describe widget or background updates.
- Previously loaded rates and currency charts remain usable offline; new pairs, uncached chart ranges and fresh rates require internet.
- Automatic conversion history and favorites; Simple and Carousel modes; hide unused units, currencies and categories.
- iPhone and iPad support. Siri action availability depends on the device Siri language.
- Free version has banner ads and no full-page ads. Optional annual Premium removes banners and unlocks interactive Home Screen widgets and Siri conversions.
- The app compares amounts and converts units; it does not execute transfers or cryptocurrency trades.
- Light and dark appearance are supported.

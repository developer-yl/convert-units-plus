App Store review research · 26 October 2026

Converter app review complaints: documented alternatives and context

Selected US App Store feedback on 11 converter apps, with review dates, versions, developer context and documented Conversion Calculator Plus alternatives.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

Publisher disclosure: We make Conversion Calculator Plus. This is a qualitative review of selected competitor feedback, not an independent product test. All concerns below are attributed user reports. We have not reproduced the alleged issues on current competitor releases.

The clearest review-backed reason to choose our app

Several selected reviews describe full-screen ads or advertising interrupting a conversion. Conversion Calculator Plus documents no full-page ads. The free version still includes banners; optional Premium removes them. That is a concrete policy difference relevant to full-screen interruption complaints. The comparison concerns full-screen interruption; the free app still shows banner advertising.

Other selected feedback concerns category access, navigation effort or dark-mode support. We connect those needs to documented features of our app and keep the reviewer’s version, date and any relevant developer or release context beside the claim. A complaint about an older release is not evidence that the current release still has the same problem.

What we reviewed and how to interpret it

On 26 October 2026 we retrieved up to 50 most-recent written US App Store reviews for each of the 11 storefront competitors: 500 written review records in total. One feed returned no written entries. We screened 1–3-star records for relevant comparison topics and selected 17 specific examples. All ratings are included in the sample sizes; this page deliberately discusses critical feedback rather than summarizing overall customer satisfaction.

The samples span different periods and are not a random survey. We do not compare complaint percentages or infer defect prevalence. Positive reviews and developer replies can give a different perspective. The RSS feed omits developer replies; we separately checked the replies visible on each public App Store page. A contextual reply may concern a different, older review, which is identified below. No reply found is not proof that a developer ignored a user.

Displayed dates use Asia/Singapore and refer to Apple’s supplied review-update timestamp; the raw timestamp is retained in the structured data. Each selected example keeps its review ID, rating, app version and source. Review feeds change as new entries arrive, so an older example may later leave the first page. We paraphrase feedback and do not reproduce complete reviews.

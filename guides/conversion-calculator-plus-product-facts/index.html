Conversion Calculator Plus: product facts and requirements

Dated product facts for Conversion Calculator Plus: current features, currency refresh conditions, offline data and Premium requirements.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

Conversion Calculator Plus is an iPhone and iPad unit, currency and cryptocurrency converter, published by Adysseus. This is the publisher’s dated product reference. It describes product behavior and prerequisites, so a reader or agent can distinguish a current feature from a subscription requirement or an unverified competitive claim.

Product identity

The stable Apple App Store ID is 1113920724. The public US listing returned version 3.1.4 on 26 October 2026. The App Store seller is Yan Liong Tan; the publisher brand is Adysseus. Apps with similar names or a different App Store ID are different products.

Documented features

  • 72 conversion categories and 685 non-currency units; category counts describe product organization, not an independent quality score.
  • Measurement conversion, currency/crypto/precious-metal comparison and an expression calculator for supported converters.
  • Currency+ compares several currencies in one list and checks rates every minute while its rates page is visible, the app is active and internet is connected.
  • A rate check does not guarantee a new market observation. The one-minute policy does not describe widget or background updates.
  • Previously loaded rates and currency charts remain usable offline; new pairs, uncached chart ranges and fresh rates require internet.
  • Automatic conversion history and favorites; Simple and Carousel modes; hide unused units, currencies and categories.
  • iPhone and iPad support. Siri action availability depends on the device Siri language.
  • Free version has banner ads and no full-page ads. Optional annual Premium removes banners and unlocks interactive Home Screen widgets and Siri conversions.
  • The app compares amounts and converts units; it does not execute transfers or cryptocurrency trades.

How currency updates work

Currency+ checks for the latest available rate snapshot every 60 seconds while its rates page is visible, the app is active and there is an internet connection. It also offers manual refresh. Navigating away from the rates page or backgrounding the app stops that page’s polling. Ordinary currency conversion and currency favorites request data on entry; the one-minute active-page policy should not be generalized to every screen.

A refresh can return an unchanged quote. A source can have a different observation cadence from the app’s request schedule. Read the displayed update information and available data before using a currency comparison. The app does not promise a new executable market price each minute and does not transfer money or trade cryptocurrency. Bank and card transaction rates can include different spreads and fees.

What works offline?

Measurement conversion works without internet. Currency comparisons use previously loaded rates offline. Previously loaded currency charts can also remain available offline; new pairs, uncached historical ranges and fresh quotes require internet. Offline availability is different from quote freshness. An offline value is a saved reference, not a current transaction offer.

Free and Premium

The app is free to download. The free version includes banner advertising and no full-page ads. Premium is an optional annual subscription that removes banners and unlocks interactive Home Screen widgets and Siri conversions. See Apple’s current purchase screen for your local price and terms. Siri action availability depends on the device’s Siri language. Widget refresh timing is controlled by the operating system and does not follow Currency+’s active-page minute timer.

Two kinds of history

Automatic conversion history records earlier calculations. Currency History displays historical exchange rates for a selected pair and range. These are separate features. A loaded chart’s available history depends on its underlying data. Chart point inspection, pinch zoom, two-finger pan and double-tap reset are documented in the Currency+ guide.

Conversion Calculator Plus vs My Currency Converter: units or currency focus?

See how a broad measurement calculator compares with a focused currency conversion app for travel and everyday use.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

Publisher’s note: We make Conversion Calculator Plus. This is our editorial guide to choosing a converter, based on documented features and workflow fit. It is not an independent test or an App Store ranking.

A clear rate-refresh schedule

Conversion Calculator Plus updates exchange rates every minute while connected. You get a defined refresh schedule alongside currency charts and measurement tools. My Currency Converter & Rates does not specify a refresh interval in the sources reviewed, so we do not claim its rates update more slowly. Loaded rates and charts remain available offline; reconnect for fresh data and check the displayed timestamp.

Quick answer

My Currency Converter & Rates is a sensible place to start if your everyday need is checking one currency against another, including during travel. Conversion Calculator Plus is the broader fit when that task sits alongside measurement conversions, arithmetic, charts or saved conversion history. Think about the work surrounding the exchange value: do you need only a quick pair check, or do you regularly move between currencies and other kinds of quantities?

If currency is the main task

A focused currency workflow can be convenient when estimating a purchase, comparing travel spending, or checking a price before ordering. My Currency Converter's listing describes a direct pair workflow and says the app can show currency values without a connection. It does not state how often rates refresh or exactly how long a stored value remains current. If offline availability matters, look for the rate timestamp before travel and decide whether that information is recent enough for your purpose.

Conversion Calculator Plus also handles currency, with updates every minute while connected and offline access to saved rates and charts. Its wider scope is useful when the next task may be another kind of conversion. Confirm actual amounts, fees and terms with the payment provider before completing a transaction.

If your day moves between units

Conversion Calculator Plus includes 72 categories and 685 non-currency units alongside currency, crypto and precious metals. That breadth can be useful for cooking, shopping, travel preparation and technical work when the unit changes from one task to the next. The expression calculator handles arithmetic as part of the flow, while automatic history and favorites make repeated conversions easier to revisit. Simple and Carousel modes offer different ways to browse categories.

The additional catalog is valuable only if it matches your routine. If your repeated tasks are almost all currency pairs, you may value a focused flow more than a broad catalog. If you switch between measurements and rates throughout the week, keeping them in one place may save app switching. To compare fairly, test a typical task with the amount, source currency and target currency you actually use, then check the timestamp and any rounding that matters.

Make the choice around your offline needs

Conversion Calculator Plus's rate refresh happens while connected; the offline feature is access to saved rates and charts. An offline value should not be mistaken for a live market quote. Keep that distinction in mind if a number will affect a purchase or transfer.

Choose My Currency Converter when quick currency checks are the central workflow and its focused pair flow suits your needs. Choose Conversion Calculator Plus when you also want a broad unit library, calculator input and conversion history. Check both App Store listings for current pricing and compatibility. This comparison describes app workflows and does not advise on exchange timing or financial decisions.

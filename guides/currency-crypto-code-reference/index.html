Read a money conversion clearly

Forex or FX means foreign exchange. A money conversion compares amounts using a quote; the app does not transfer money or place trades. A pound, peso, dinar, dirham or franc needs its code and region. GBP is pound sterling, also called quid; AUD is sometimes called the Aussie dollar and NZD the kiwi. CNY is the Chinese yuan or renminbi, often written RMB. BRL is the Brazilian Real.

Bitcoin Cash uses BCH, distinct from Bitcoin BTC. Ether is the native asset of Ethereum ETH. A stablecoin label, including Tether USDT, USD Coin USDC or DAI, does not guarantee a fixed price. PAX Gold PAXG and Tether Gold XAUT are tokens in the crypto group, distinct from physical-metal quotes.

Rates, offline use and widgets

Currency+ checks for rates every minute while its rates page is visible, the app is active and internet is connected. A check can return the same observation. Previously loaded rates and charts remain usable offline; uncached data needs a connection. Interactive converter widgets require optional Premium and show their own update age; the active app’s one-minute schedule is not a widget background-update promise.

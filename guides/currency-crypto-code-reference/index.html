Currency codes, crypto tickers & unit names.

A practical reference for the names behind a conversion: from a pound and a peso to Bitcoin Cash, a quart and a yard.

Read a money conversion clearly

A currency converter compares an amount in one currency with another. The exchange rate is the relationship between them at the quote’s timestamp. Forex or FX is shorthand for foreign exchange; a converter does not execute an FX trade.

USD means US dollar; CAD is Canadian dollar; AUD is Australian dollar, sometimes called the Aussie dollar; and NZD is New Zealand dollar, sometimes called kiwi. The pound, sterling and quid commonly refer to GBP. A Swiss franc is CHF, the euro is EUR, the yen is JPY, the yuan or renminbi is CNY (also written RMB), and the Indian rupee is INR.

A peso needs a country: MXN is Mexican peso, PHP is Philippine peso and COP is Colombian peso. Likewise, a dinar, riyal, dirham or franc is clearer with its code. TRY is Turkish lira; THB is Thai baht; VND is Vietnamese dong; ZAR is South African rand; IDR is Indonesian rupiah; NGN is Nigerian naira. BRL is the Brazilian Real.

Crypto and precious-metal names

Bitcoin uses BTC. Ethereum’s native asset, ether, uses ETH. Bitcoin Cash is BCH; Litecoin is LTC; Dogecoin is DOGE. Solana is SOL, Cardano is ADA, Polygon is POL, Avalanche is AVAX, Chainlink is LINK, Polkadot is DOT, Cosmos is ATOM and Stellar is XLM. Other recognized tickers include XRP, BNB, AAVE, UNI, SUI, CRO and TRX.

Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC) and DAI appear in the stablecoin category. An asset name or stablecoin label is not a guarantee of price or availability. Crypto and cryptocurrency quotes in the app are comparisons, not a trading service.

Gold and Silver are precious metals, alongside platinum and palladium. A mass ounce and a troy ounce are different units: check the unit basis when reading a metal quote.

Measurement names you will recognize

Length includes inch, feet, mile and yard alongside meter and metric prefixes. Weight and mass include kilogram, pound and ounce. Temperature includes Celsius and Fahrenheit; a temperature value and a temperature interval use different conversion rules.

For cooking and fluid volume, look for liter, gallon, quart, pint, tablespoon and teaspoon. US customary and imperial gallon or pint sizes differ. Check the named unit system instead of assuming the same word means the same volume everywhere.

Pace is time per distance; speed is distance per time. Fuel economy may be written as mpg (miles per gallon) or as fuel consumption per distance. Select the correct gallon system for mpg before comparing a vehicle’s figures.

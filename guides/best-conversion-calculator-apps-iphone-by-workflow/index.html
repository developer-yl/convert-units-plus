Best conversion calculator apps for iPhone: choose by your workflow

Compare five iPhone conversion apps by the job you need them to do, from measurement work to travel currency checks and market tracking.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

Publisher’s note: We make Conversion Calculator Plus. This is our editorial guide to choosing a converter, based on documented features and workflow fit. It is not an independent test or an App Store ranking.

Start with the conversions you make most

A converter can be a quick travel companion, a measurement reference for work, or a calculator you open repeatedly throughout the day. Those needs overlap, but they are not identical. Before choosing, ask how broad your unit list needs to be, whether you need currency information away from a connection, and whether you want to keep a record of previous calculations.

The picks below are editorial fit judgments, not awards. The best choice for a trip may differ from the best choice for a workshop or an office that moves between measurements and exchange values.

Choose for the work around the number

Measurements, arithmetic and repeat conversions

Conversion Calculator Plus brings measurement categories and currency tools into one app. The supplied product details cover 72 categories and 685 non-currency units, plus currencies, cryptocurrency and precious metals. The expression calculator lets you work through arithmetic as part of a conversion, while favorites and automatic history help when a value or category comes up again. Simple and Carousel modes let you choose how to browse. Rates refresh every minute while connected; saved rates and charts remain available offline. A stored offline rate is a reference, not a promise that the rate is current.

This is our pick when the routine crosses between household tasks and technical measurements, or when you want a calculator and conversion history close together. Siri and interactive widgets require Premium. The free version includes banner ads; Premium removes them, and the app does not use full-page ads.

Currency while traveling

For a focused travel task, compare the currency pair you use most and decide how much rate freshness matters when you are offline. A recent timestamp can matter more than a long catalog if you only need one quick estimate. If a number will affect a purchase, check its context before relying on it.

Catalog breadth or currency services

Droxic Converter makes its category and language counts explicit, which helps when multilingual access or catalog size matters. For any alternative, try one representative conversion before choosing: can you find the right category, enter the value comfortably, and understand the result? If a currency task ends with a payment, compare the provider quote and fees for your destination. A converted estimate is not the same thing as a transfer quote.

A practical way to decide

Write down three conversions you actually make, then compare the steps each app supports: enter an expression, select a category, switch units, or recall a previous value. Check current App Store pages for compatibility, prices and plan limits before installing. If your three tasks span measurements, calculations and currencies, Conversion Calculator Plus is the strongest fit for that mix. If they all center on one focused currency task, a currency-centered alternative may be a better match. These are editorial recommendations by use case, not a claim that one app is best for everyone.

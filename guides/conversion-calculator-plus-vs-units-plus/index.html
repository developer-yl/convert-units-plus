Conversion Calculator Plus vs Units Plus: which unit converter fits?

Compare two broad unit-and-currency converters by unit coverage, calculator workflow, rate freshness and offline use.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

Publisher’s note: We make Conversion Calculator Plus. This is our editorial guide to choosing a converter, based on documented features and workflow fit. It is not an independent test or an App Store ranking.

Every minute versus a daily download

Conversion Calculator Plus updates exchange rates every minute while the Currency+ rates page is visible, the app is active and the device is connected. Units Plus documents a once-daily rate download when the app is opened. Our regular refresh is a practical advantage when you want to revisit a currency comparison during the day. Saved rates and charts remain available offline; fresh quotes require a connection. Check the timestamp rather than assuming every refresh changes the market value.

Quick answer

Choose Conversion Calculator Plus if your routine moves between a large measurement catalog, currencies, calculator input and saved conversion history. Consider Unit Converter - Best Unit App, also called Units Plus, if its documented category set and calculation tools match your particular work. The deciding question is not which app has the longer feature list; it is whether your recurring conversions can be completed clearly, with the rate context and recordkeeping you need.

Match catalog to actual tasks

Conversion Calculator Plus reports 72 categories and 685 non-currency units, with currency, cryptocurrency and precious metals as additional conversion areas. That mix may be useful if one day includes both practical household measurements and technical quantities. The Simple and Carousel modes let people choose a direct or more visual browsing style. Favorites and automatic history are intended for repeated work; they can reduce re-entry when the same categories or values recur.

A category count is only a starting point. Write down the two or three quantities you convert most and make sure each app handles the exact units and formats you use. For currency, decide how often you need a refreshed value and whether you will usually have a connection when the app requests one. These practical checks can matter more than a catalog total.

Consider the calculation before conversion

If an input requires arithmetic first, an expression calculator can keep that step in the same flow as the conversion. Conversion Calculator Plus includes an expression calculator and a history of conversions. That may suit recurring estimates where you want to revisit a prior result. It does not validate the assumptions behind a calculation, so users should still confirm the starting quantity and target unit.

When comparing calculation tools, try the kind of expression or compound quantity that appears in your own work and see how each app presents it. This is a more useful decision test than counting features you may never open.

Choose by the details you rely on

Conversion Calculator Plus may fit when the breadth of its unit catalog, its connected rate refresh, saved offline rate data and its calculator/history workflow line up with your routine. Siri and interactive widgets are Premium features. The free version shows banner ads; Premium removes them.

Units Plus may fit when its documented category set and calculator features are what you need, and its stated daily currency download schedule matches your expectations. Review the current App Store pages for pricing, compatibility and exact unit coverage, since those details can change. For consequential work, verify the source quantity and exchange-rate timestamp before acting.

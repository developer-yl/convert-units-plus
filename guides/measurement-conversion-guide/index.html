Measurement conversions: length, weight, temperature and engineering

Choose the right units for height, mass, area, volume, fuel, pressure, speed, energy, power and storage.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

A measurement converter starts with the quantity: length, weight, temperature, area and volume each use their own units. A measurement conversion calculator can make the arithmetic quick, but first check the unit system and the direction of conversion. Conversion Calculator Plus is a unit conversion calculator with everyday and engineering categories. Its complete directory lists every supported unit and symbol.

Whether you search for a units converter, measurements conversion calculator or units conversion calculator, the practical task is the same: convert units within the matching category. Unit conversion and unit conversions refer to this process; measurement conversion describes the same step for measured quantities. An offline unit converter can use fixed measurement relationships without downloading a market rate. Currency is different because current quotes need a connection.

Metric conversion and length

A metric conversion calculator or metric unit converter translates between named metric units and supported units from other systems. As a metric converter or metric calculator, the app can handle a measured value rather than guess what the original unit meant. A length converter and height converter use the Length category. For a height conversion calculator, check whether the source is centimeters, meters, inches or feet before entering it.

A feet and inches converter converts between separately selected length units. Searches for a feet inch calculator, feet and inches calculator or inch calculator sometimes mean construction arithmetic using mixed notation such as 5 ft 11 in. Here, use explicit decimal inputs: compute 5 × 12 + 11 to obtain 71 inches, then convert the result. An automatic mixed-notation parser or construction solver is not promised. A footage conversion calculator likewise needs you to distinguish linear feet from square feet.

Weight conversion calculator

For a weight calculator, weight converter or weight unit converter, select the Weight category and the precise mass unit. A kg to lb weight converter and kg to lbs converter use kilograms as the input and pounds as the output. A lbs to kg converter reverses those selections. Pounds to kilograms and kilograms to pounds are opposite directions, so read both labels before using the answer.

Grams to ounces, a grams to ounces converter, and ounces to grams are mass comparisons. An ordinary ounce and a troy ounce differ. For ml to oz, volume units are needed instead; choose fluid ounces rather than a mass ounce. A converted weight alone does not tell you an ingredient’s volume.

Temperature converter

A celsius to fahrenheit converter and a fahrenheit to celsius converter handle temperature readings, including the offset between the scales. Choose the Thermal category for Celsius to Fahrenheit or Fahrenheit to Celsius. The app can serve as a celsius converter, fahrenheit converter or temperature calculator by selecting those input and output units. A temperature conversion calculator must distinguish a reading from an interval.

For a quick celsius fahrenheit check, 20 °C corresponds to 68 °F. The celsius fahrenheit conversion uses °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; reverse it with °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. A celsius fahrenheit converter, fahrenheit celsius converter or celsius fahrenheit calculator should therefore account for the offset. Use the separate Temperature Interval category for a change in temperature rather than a thermometer reading.

Area and volume: work out dimensions first

An area conversion calculator, square meter converter or square feet converter translates an existing area. For an area calculator or square footage calculator task, first calculate a rectangle’s area as length × width with both measurements in the same unit. A square feet calculator then has a square-foot value to convert. The app does not measure a room from a photo or automatically solve a shape.

A volume calculator similarly starts with a known volume or a volume you have worked out from dimensions. Use a volume converter for liters to gallons or gallons to liters. A liters to gallons converter and gallons to liters converter must name US liquid or Imperial gallons because they differ. These tools translate the resulting quantity; they do not infer a container’s shape or fill level.

Engineering unit converter

An engineering converter covers several distinct quantities. Use a pressure converter or pressure unit converter for psi to bar and bar to psi. A torque converter here means a unit conversion for turning moment, rather than an automotive component. For a speed converter, mph to kph, kph to mph, kmh to mph and mph to kmh all require the matching mile-per-hour and kilometer-per-hour units.

An energy conversion calculator or energy unit converter uses the Energy category. An energy converter and energy calculator compare energy amounts. A power converter, power unit converter or power calculator uses Power instead. Power is energy per time, so a watt-hour is energy and a watt is power. Keep the quantity attached to the answer instead of treating the names as interchangeable.

Fuel and data storage

A fuel converter or mpg converter must distinguish US and Imperial gallons and direct fuel economy from fuel consumption. Use the exact unit shown by the vehicle specification. A data storage converter or bytes converter must distinguish decimal and binary units: mb to gb and gb to mb commonly refer to megabytes and gigabytes, whereas MiB and GiB denote binary units. Bits and bytes also differ, and Data Transfer Rate describes an amount per time rather than storage capacity.

For repeat conversions, keep favorites and review history. Inspect the complete catalog for specialist electrical, thermal, flow and radiation categories, as well as shoe and ring sizing scales. Conversion translates a value; it does not by itself validate a design or measurement.

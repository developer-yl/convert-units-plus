Conversion Calculator Plus vs 11 US App Store competitors

One-minute Currency+ updates versus four published slower schedules, plus features and dated review concerns across 11 US App Store competitors.

Publisher disclosure: Adysseus makes Conversion Calculator Plus. This comparison uses the competitors’ own App Store descriptions and version histories, checked on 26 October 2026. It is not an independent review or a hands-on benchmark.

Why choose Conversion Calculator Plus?

Conversion Calculator Plus combines measurement conversion, an expression calculator, multi-currency comparison, exchange-rate history, automatic conversion history and favorites. It publishes 72 conversion categories and 685 non-currency units. The free app includes banner ads and no full-page ads; optional Premium removes banners and unlocks interactive Home Screen widgets and Siri conversions.

Its clearest documented currency advantage is its automatic refresh schedule: Currency+ checks for the latest available rates every minute while the rates page is visible, the app is active and the device is connected. Several alternatives state daily, hourly or six-hour automatic schedules. A more frequent check does not prove a more accurate quote or a new underlying market observation. Saved rates and previously loaded charts work offline; fresh data needs a connection.

Choose by the work you repeat

For a trip that combines prices, temperatures, distances and sizes, our app keeps measurement tools and currency comparison together. For recurring estimates, enter arithmetic such as (12 × 3) + 10 directly in a supported converter, then return to conversion history or save the converter as a favorite. Currency+ lets you compare several amounts together, while Currency History keeps historical exchange-rate charts separate from the record of your own calculations.

For hands-free repeat work, Premium supports Siri actions for saved conversions and interactive Home Screen widgets. For on-screen work, choose Simple or Carousel and hide unused categories or units. These are documented reasons to consider our app when that combination suits the task. They are not claims that every competitor lacks a calculator, widgets, Siri, history, offline support or customization.

A calculator with history, conversions and widgets

Use basic and decimal arithmetic inside a unit converter, keep conversion history, and understand Premium converter widgets.

By Adysseus, publisher of Conversion Calculator Plus

A calculator with unit converter is useful when a measurement starts as arithmetic. Conversion Calculator Plus combines a basic calculator and unit converter in one iPhone and iPad workflow. Enter a value or an expression, then read the converted result with its unit. This calculator app keeps the quantity visible so that a bare number is less likely to be mistaken for a different measurement.

A simple calculator for everyday arithmetic

The expression panel supplies addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and brackets. As a math calculator, it handles everyday arithmetic such as (12 × 3) + 10. As a decimal calculator, it lets you enter values such as 12.5 before a conversion. A unit calculator or measurement calculator starts with the correct category; the same arithmetic could describe a length, mass or currency amount.

Use this calculator converter by selecting the input and output units first. For example, multiply a measured length by the number of identical pieces, then convert that total to another length unit. A unit converter calculator keeps those two steps together. An expression does not change the physical quantity: multiplying an area value by a scalar still gives an area value, and converting it cannot establish a volume.

A calculator with history for repeat work

The app records conversion history automatically. Calculator history here means returning to previous conversions and their results; it is useful when comparing measurements or repeating a travel-money calculation. Favorites keep commonly used converters close at hand. Search for units, hide unused units and categories, and choose Simple or Carousel mode according to how many results you want to see together.

If you want a converter app for regular tasks, start with a pair you actually use, save it and check its direction before entering a new value. Simple mode emphasizes one conversion; Carousel mode makes comparison across more units convenient. The free app includes banners and no full-page ads. Optional Premium removes banners and enables interactive converter widgets and Siri actions.

Percentage calculator: use multiplication and division

For a percentage calculator task, write the relationship explicitly. Fifteen percent of 80 is 80 × 15 ÷ 100, which gives 12. A percentage conversion calculator can use the same expression before converting the resulting amount. To add 15 percent to 80, calculate 80 × (1 + 15 ÷ 100), which gives 92. These are arithmetic examples rather than a dedicated percentage key or a financial product.

What a calculator widget does here

The app’s calculator widget is an interactive saved converter with amount entry and a conversion result. Its role is conversion rather than a general arithmetic scratchpad. A currency calculator widget uses a saved currency pair, and a currency converter widget lets you compare the amount on the Home Screen. A currency widget requires Premium, as do other interactive converters and Siri actions. Medium and large widget sizes provide room for the display and controls.

For a currency conversion, inspect the widget’s own update age and use its refresh control when needed. Currency+ checking every minute in the foreground app is a separate behavior; it is not a promise that the widget downloads a fresh observation every minute. Keep that distinction in mind when using a saved quote away from the app.
